🤖 Autopilot

Every day at your chosen time you get a notification. Tap it and keep the screen on: the app checks your stats, learns from them, then writes, illustrates, voices and renders the next part. When it's done, Share opens so you can post it to YouTube.

Growth playbook

  1. Post as a series. Part 1, 2, 3… with a cliffhanger every time. Put them in a playlist and link it in the pinned comment.
  2. Hook in 2 seconds. The first caption has to raise a question.
  3. Same cast every episode. Viewers come back for characters they recognise; your Cast tab locks their looks.
  4. One Short a day, same time. Consistency beats volume.
  5. Comments → next episode. Paste what viewers ask for into “Previous parts” and the next part delivers it.
  6. Monetization: YouTube rewards original, story-driven series over mass-produced clips, so keep editing scenes and adding your own twists.
